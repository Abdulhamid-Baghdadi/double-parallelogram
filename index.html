Double parallelogram arm

How palletising arms keep their motors on the base and their tool level, with two four-bar linkages and no extra motor.

What it is

A parallelogram is a four-bar linkage whose opposite links are equal. Its coupler always stays parallel to the frame, so a bar that turns at one end turns the same way at the other. Two of them in series give the classic palletising arm:

  1. Drive parallelogram (orange). Servo B sits on the base, coaxial with servo A. It turns a short crank. A rod as long as the upper arm joins the crank to a lever on the back of the forearm. The forearm then always points the way the crank points, so the forearm angle from the ground equals servo B's angle, whatever servo A does.
  2. Levelling parallelogram (green). A fixed bar on the base, a rod parallel to the upper arm, a triangle at the elbow, and a long link parallel to the forearm hold the flange in one orientation. The tool stays level over the whole workspace with no motor at the wrist.

The result: both heavy motors stay on the base, the joints are decoupled (each servo sets one absolute angle), and lifting a load keeps it level. The cost is extra links and two singular poses where crank and upper arm line up (dead centre); the readout turns red near them.

Equations

Upper arm L1, forearm L2, angles from the horizontal. Forward kinematics of the wrist:

x = L₁ cos q₁ + L₂ cos q₂, y = L₁ sin q₁ + L₂ sin q₂

Inverse, with d = √(x² + y²), α = atan2(y, x), c = (d² + L₁² − L₂²) / (2 L₁ d):

q₁ = α + acos c, q₂ = atan2(y − L₁ sin q₁, x − L₁ cos q₁)

Keep q₁ − q₂ roughly between 25° and 155° so the drive parallelogram never reaches dead centre.

Built examples

  • EEZYbotARM Mk2 by Carlo Franciscone, 3D printed, three hobby servos plus a gripper servo, frame based on a palletising robot: Instructables, MyMiniFactory; variant Armstrong.
  • ROBOTIS OpenManipulator Link, open-source parallel-linkage arm for palletising, Dynamixel servos.
  • Duet3D, 4-axis palletized robot: firmware kinematics for a two-stage parallelogram arm whose end platform stays parallel to the base.
  • Bittle-servo crane: a two-joint version with one parallelogram, driven by Petoi Bittle servos.
